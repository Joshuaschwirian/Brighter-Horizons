01 / OUR VISION

A place to call home.
A life to call your own.

Every adult with a developmental disability deserves a real home and the opportunity to live a full, meaningful life.

Brighter Horizons is a care company in development, planning small group homes for adults with developmental disabilities in real neighborhoods. Our vision begins with the individual—their relationships, health, choices, and everyday joy.

We’re laying a lasting foundation for thoughtful care. Our goal is to create homes where each person has their own space, familiar routines, and opportunities to keep growing and experiencing life.

How we’re thinking about home

02 / OUR APPROACH

Thoughtfully envisioned.
Centered on people.

Our plans are guided by a simple idea: a meaningful life is made up of meaningful everyday moments.

⌂01

Real homes.

Small houses in real neighborhoods, with personal spaces and routines shaped around the people who live there. We envision community as part of everyday life.

✳02

People who care.

Great care begins with supported caregivers. Our plans prioritize training, continuity, and a workplace where Direct Support Professionals can build lasting relationships.

◎03

A full life.

Relationships, health, activity, choice, and purpose. We aim to plan around each individual and measure success by quality of life, beyond the basics of compliance.

THE STORY BEHIND THE VISION

A lifelong connection.
A deeply personal purpose.

A NOTE FROM OUR FOUNDER
“My sister is the reason.”

Growing up helping care for an older sister with developmental disabilities made this mission personal long before Brighter Horizons began. That connection extended to Special Olympics, first as a unified basketball partner at age five and later as a coach.

The search for a home for a loved one brought the vision into focus: a place each resident can truly call their own, with the support and opportunity to keep growing and experiencing life.

That lived experience is the heart of Brighter Horizons—and the reason quality of life will always come first.
