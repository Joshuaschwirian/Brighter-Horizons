THE REASON BEHIND IT ALL

“My sister
is the reason.”

Growing up helping care for an older sister with developmental disabilities made this mission personal long before Brighter Horizons began. At age five, our founder joined her Special Olympics basketball team as a unified partner, later becoming a coach.

Searching for a home for a loved one brought the vision into focus: a place each resident can truly call their own, with the support and opportunity to keep growing and experiencing life.

That lived experience is the heart of Brighter Horizons.

OUR MISSION

A real home.
A life of your own.

Every adult with a developmental disability deserves a home and a life full of dignity, connection, and joy. We’re laying the foundation for small group homes where individual preferences, relationships, and everyday experiences come first.

How we’re thinking about home
